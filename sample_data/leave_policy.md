# Nexora Technologies — Leave & Time-Off Policy (FY 2025-26)

## 1. Leave Types

| Leave Type | Annual Quota | Carry Forward | Encashable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave (CL) | 12 days | No | No |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 10 days | Up to 5 days | No |
| Earned/Privilege Leave (EL) | 18 days | Up to 30 days | Yes (max 15 days/yr) |
| Maternity Leave | 26 weeks | N/A | N/A |
| Paternity Leave | 15 days | N/A | N/A |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 days | N/A | N/A |
| Sabbatical (post 5 yrs tenure) | Up to 90 days unpaid | N/A | N/A |

## 2. Accrual Rules

- CL and SL are credited at the start of the calendar year (Jan 1).
- EL accrues monthly at 1.5 days/month, credited on the last working day of each month.
- New joiners get pro-rated CL/SL based on joining month; EL accrual starts from month 1.

## 3. Leave Approval Workflow

1. Employee applies via HR portal at least 2 working days in advance (except sick leave).
2. Reporting manager approves/rejects within 24 hours.
3. If unapproved after 48 hours, leave auto-escalates to the manager's manager.
4. Leaves during active sprint/release freeze weeks require additional Engineering Lead sign-off.

## 4. Sick Leave Special Cases

- Sick leave beyond 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate uploaded to the HR portal.
- Sick leave taken on a day adjacent to a public holiday or weekend (>2 instances/quarter) may trigger an HR review for pattern abuse.
- COVID/infectious illness leave does not count against the SL quota; it's tracked separately as Health Emergency Leave (up to 10 additional days).

## 5. Work From Home (WFH) Policy

- Standard hybrid: 3 days in-office (Tue/Wed/Thu), 2 days WFH per week.
- Full WFH requires Director-level approval and is reviewed quarterly.
- WFH is not counted as leave and does not require advance approval, but must be logged in the attendance portal by 10 AM.

## 6. Negative Leave Balance

- Employees may go up to -3 days negative on CL only, adjusted against next month's accrual.
- SL and EL cannot go negative; unapproved absence beyond quota is marked Loss of Pay (LOP).

## 7. Notice Period & Leave Encashment on Exit

- Standard notice period: 60 days for Band 3+, 30 days for Band 1-2.
- Unused EL (up to accumulated cap of 30 days) is encashed at last drawn basic salary rate on full and final settlement.
- CL and SL are forfeited on exit, not encashed.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: Can I take CL and SL together for a long weekend?**
Yes, but combining more than 4 consecutive days requires manager pre-approval regardless of leave type mix.

**Q: Does a public holiday falling within an approved leave block count against quota?**
No, public holidays within a leave span are excluded from the deducted day count.

**Q: What happens if I resign during a release freeze?**
Notice period still applies as per band; however, last 2 weeks of notice may be adjusted to garden leave at manager discretion.
