# Nexora Technologies — Salary Hike Policy & Guidelines (FY 2025-26)

## 1. Overview

This document defines how annual salary hikes are calculated at Nexora Technologies. Hikes are determined using a combination of experience band, performance rating, skill relevance, and business impact. This policy applies to all full-time engineering, product, and design employees.

## 2. Experience-Based Hike Bands

Base hike percentage is determined primarily by years of experience (YOE) at the company, not total career experience.

| Experience Band | Years at Company | Base Hike Range |
|---|---|---|
| Band 1 (Fresher) | 0–1 yr | 8% – 12% |
| Band 2 (Junior) | 1–3 yrs | 10% – 15% |
| Band 3 (Mid) | 3–5 yrs | 12% – 18% |
| Band 4 (Senior) | 5–8 yrs | 15% – 22% |
| Band 5 (Staff/Lead) | 8+ yrs | 18% – 28% |

Employees who joined mid-year receive a pro-rated first review.

## 3. Performance Rating Multiplier

Each employee's appraisal rating (1 to 5 scale) applies a multiplier on top of the base hike band.

| Rating | Label | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding | 1.4x |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | 1.2x |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | 1.0x |
| 2 | Needs Improvement | 0.5x |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | 0x (no hike, PIP triggered) |

Example: A Band 3 employee (base 15%) with a rating of 4 receives 15% × 1.2 = 18% hike.

## 4. Achievement-Based Bonus Increments

On top of the base × multiplier calculation, specific achievements add a flat bonus percentage:

- **Shipped a major feature/product independently**: +2%
- **Led a cross-team initiative or mentored 2+ juniors**: +1.5%
- **Earned a relevant certification** (AWS, Kubernetes CKA, security certs like OSCP, cloud architect certs): +1%
- **Filed a patent or published research adopted internally**: +3%
- **Critical incident resolution / production save**: +1%
- **Open source contribution recognized by the company**: +0.5%

Bonus increments are capped at a combined +5% per cycle regardless of how many achievements are logged.

## 5. Skill Relevance Adjustment

If an employee upskills into a business-critical area during the review cycle, an additional adjustment applies:

- **High-demand skill acquired** (e.g., AI/ML, cybersecurity, cloud infra, distributed systems): +1.5%
- **Internal tool/certification track completed** (e.g., completed Anthropic API / agent-building coursework used in production work): +1%
- **Cross-functional skill (e.g., backend engineer picking up DevOps/CI-CD ownership)**: +1%

## 6. Promotion Hikes (Separate from Annual Hike)

Promotions are evaluated separately from the annual cycle and carry their own hike, added on top of (not merged with) the annual hike:

| Promotion Type | Additional Hike |
|---|---|
| Individual Contributor level-up (e.g., SDE-1 → SDE-2) | +10% – 15% |
| Senior to Lead | +15% – 20% |
| Lead to Manager (people management track) | +18% – 25% |
| Manager to Director | +20% – 30% |

## 7. Retention Adjustments

If an employee has a competing offer or flight risk is flagged by their manager, HR may apply a one-time retention adjustment of 5%–15%, approved by the department head, independent of the standard formula above.

## 8. Cap and Exceptions

- Maximum combined hike (base + performance + achievements + skill relevance) in a single cycle is capped at **35%**, except for promotion cases.
- Hikes below 3 months of tenure in the current role are not eligible unless tied to a promotion.
- All hikes require manager approval and are reviewed by HR before disbursement in the April payroll cycle.

## 9. Sample Calculation Walkthroughs

**Case A — Priya, Backend Engineer**
- Band 3 (4 years at company), base hike: 15%
- Rating: 4 (Exceeds Expectations) → multiplier 1.2x → 18%
- Achievement: Led migration of legacy service to microservices (+2%)
- Skill: Completed cloud architect certification (+1%)
- **Total hike: 21%**

**Case B — Arjun, DevOps Lead**
- Band 4 (6 years at company), base hike: 18%
- Rating: 5 (Outstanding) → multiplier 1.4x → 25.2%
- Achievement: Resolved critical production incident (+1%), mentored 3 juniors (+1.5%)
- Combined achievement bonus capped at +5%, so applies full +2.5%
- **Total hike: 27.7%**

**Case C — Sneha, Fresher SDE**
- Band 1 (8 months at company), base hike: 10%
- Rating: 3 (Meets Expectations) → multiplier 1.0x → 10%
- No major achievements logged this cycle
- **Total hike: 10%**

**Case D — Rahul, Security Engineer (promoted mid-cycle)**
- Band 3 (3.5 years), base hike: 13%
- Rating: 4 → multiplier 1.2x → 15.6%
- Achievement: Filed internal patent for vulnerability detection method (+3%)
- Promotion: SDE-2 → Senior SDE-2 (+12%, added separately)
- **Total: 18.6% annual hike + 12% promotion hike**

## 10. FAQ

**Q: Does referral bonus count toward hike calculation?**
No, referral bonuses are separate one-time payouts and do not affect the annual hike percentage.

**Q: Can a rating-2 employee still get achievement bonuses?**
No. A rating of 2 or below excludes the employee from achievement and skill bonuses until the next cycle.

**Q: Is the hike calculated on CTC or base salary?**
All percentages in this document apply to fixed base salary, not total CTC (which includes variable pay and benefits).
